## What I'm building

**HONDO - Hybrid Operations Network Diagnostic Orchestrator** - is an AI-powered diagnostic and workforce-development platform for network infrastructure professionals, from field technicians to engineers and technical analysts.

The operating idea is simple: pair the person standing in front of the equipment with an always-available AI infrastructure analyst. HONDO brings the active job, device data, network evidence, technical knowledge, and approved tools into one conversation so the technician can ask better questions, understand what the system is showing, and move toward a competent resolution.

For infrastructure contractors and service organizations, the AI advantage is confidence at the point of work. A developing technician does not have to troubleshoot alone or wait until an expert becomes available. HONDO is being built to help interpret evidence, explain unfamiliar systems in practical language, recommend the next safe step, and recognize when the job should be escalated. Human approval remains part of any production change.

HONDO is being developed to:

- Use AI to analyze device, network, server, log, and field observations in one evidence-based workflow
- Hold a practical analytical conversation grounded in the equipment, symptoms, history, and requirements of the active job
- Identify infrastructure weaknesses, configuration issues, likely failure points, and gaps that require more evidence
- Connect to vendor APIs and SDKs for monitoring, diagnostics, and controlled, human-approved remediation
- Explain commands, configurations, operating-system behavior, and code without losing the field context
- Give newer technicians expert-level guidance while helping experienced technicians work faster and document more consistently
- Produce clear closeout reports and deliver notifications by email or text
- Turn real service calls into guided learning without separating training from the work

HONDO is not meant to replace technicians or engineers. It is meant to increase what they can see, understand, and safely accomplish.

## Why this matters

Infrastructure work is no longer divided between "the person who pulls cable" and "the person who configures the system." Modern technicians increasingly encounter switches, servers, sensors, firmware, cloud-managed devices, logs, APIs, and security requirements on the same job.

That creates an opportunity to cultivate a broader kind of skilled worker: someone who can build the physical path, validate connectivity, configure the connected system, re-image or update a device, interpret diagnostic output, and communicate exactly what changed.

HONDO is aimed at infrastructure contractors, staffing partners, cable and broadband service organizations, and the technicians they deploy. For companies operating across many sites and skill levels, the value is consistency: better questions in the field, clearer escalation, repeatable changes, stronger documentation, and training that follows the technician into the job.

The need is practical, not theoretical:

- [CISA emphasizes asset visibility, vulnerability detection, and configuration management](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/directives/bod-23-01-improving-asset-visibility-and-vulnerability-detection-federal-networks) as foundations for reducing network risk.
- [CISA's communications-infrastructure guidance](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/enhanced-visibility-and-hardening-guidance-communications-infrastructure) calls for monitoring network changes, centrally managing configurations, patching devices, and using disciplined change management.
- [NIST describes secure configuration management](https://www.nist.gov/publications/guide-security-focused-configuration-management-information-systems-0) as a way to monitor system configurations, support business services, and reduce organizational risk.
- [Cisco documents near-real-time streaming telemetry](https://developer.cisco.com/docs/nx-os/telemetry/) and programmable interfaces that make deeper monitoring and controlled automation possible.
- [NTIA highlights the need to train tens of thousands of broadband workers](https://broadbandusa.ntia.gov/node/9751) across roles ranging from network design to construction and fiber work.

These are the operating conditions HONDO is being built for: more connected equipment, more data, more configuration responsibility, and a workforce that needs practical ways to keep learning.
